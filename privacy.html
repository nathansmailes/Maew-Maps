Maew Maps privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Maew Maps is a community map of neighbourhood cats in Bangkok. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and how you can delete it. We keep as little as we can.

What we collect

InformationWhyWho can see it
Your email addressTo send you a sign-in codeOnly you and the Maew Maps team. Never shown in the app.
Your nickname (optional)Shown on the leaderboardEveryone
Cats you add: map pin, description, photo, place notesTo show the cat on the mapEveryone
Comments, name suggestions, votes, friendliness ratings, sightingsTo build each cat's profile and your spotter pointsComments and name suggestions are shown to everyone. Votes and ratings are shown only as totals.
Reports you sendTo remove unkind or unsafe postsOnly the Maew Maps team

Maew Maps does not track your location. Cats are placed on the map by hand. We do not show adverts and we never sell or share your information for marketing.

Please don't post photos that show people's faces, house numbers, or other private details.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the app's data and handles sign-in (servers in Asia).
  • Gmail sends the sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app.
  • MapTiler and OpenStreetMap provide the map pictures. Like any website, they see your device's internet address when the map loads.
  • Google Translate opens only if you tap a "Translate" link.

Storage on your device

The app saves a small sign-in token on your device so you stay signed in. It does not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep things

Cats that nobody has seen for a year are taken off the map. Everything else stays until you delete it or delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap your rank badge at the top, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your email, nickname, comments, votes, ratings, sightings and reports. Cats you added stay on the map for others, but are no longer linked to you.

If you can't use the app, email us from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days.

Children

Maew Maps is meant for people aged 13 and over. If you are younger, please use it with a parent or teacher.
