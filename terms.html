Maew Maps community rules

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Maew Maps is a friendly map of Bangkok's neighbourhood cats. By signing in and posting cats, photos, names or comments, you agree to these rules.

Zero tolerance for abuse

There is no place in Maew Maps for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't post anything that is:

  • rude, hateful, threatening, bullying or sexual;
  • cruel to animals, or encourages harming them;
  • spam, adverts or fake cats;
  • someone's face, home address or other private details without their permission.

Posts that break these rules are removed, and people who post them lose their account.

Report and block

  • Report: every cat, photo and comment has a ⚑ Report button. A post reported by 3 people is hidden straight away until we check it.
  • Block: tap 🚫 Block next to a comment or photo, or on a cat's page, to stop seeing anything that person posts. They aren't told. You can unblock everyone from your rank page.

We check reports within 24 hours, remove anything that breaks these rules and remove the accounts of people who posted it.

Be kind to cats

Ratings are about how a cat behaves with people, never how it looks. Please don't chase, pick up or feed cats anything that could harm them.

Contact

Questions or something urgent? Email maewmaps@gmail.com.
