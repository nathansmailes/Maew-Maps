# Maew Maps: Google Play listing

Copy each part into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

## App name (max 30 characters)
Maew Maps: Bangkok Cat Map

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Spot, name and rate the friendly street cats of Bangkok with your neighbours.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Every soi in Bangkok has its cats. Maew Maps is a community map where neighbours pin the cats they meet, so everyone can find them, look out for them and give them a name.

🗺️ A MAP OF BANGKOK'S CATS
Zoom around the city like any map app. Each pin is a cat someone has spotted, coloured to match its fur.

🐈 A PROFILE FOR EVERY CAT
Tap a pin to see the cat's description, photo, where it hangs out and when it was last seen.

😺 HOW FRIENDLY IS IT?
Rate how the cat behaved with you, from "Runs away" to "Lap cat". It's about behaviour, never looks. Every cat is a good cat.

🗳️ VOTE ON ITS NAME
Suggest names and vote. The most popular name wins.

💬 COMMENTS IN THAI OR ENGLISH
Share news, tips and sightings. One tap translates any comment between Thai and English.

🔍 NO DUPLICATES
When you add a cat, Maew Maps shows similar cats already pinned nearby, so you can log a sighting instead of adding the same cat twice.

🏅 SPOTTER RANKS
Earn points for every new cat and sighting. Climb from Kitten to Soi Scout, Alley Cat, Street Whisker, Cat Whisperer and Maew Legend, and see who tops the leaderboard.

🛡️ KIND AND SAFE
Anyone can report an unkind post, and posts with several reports are hidden. Cats that nobody has seen for a year leave the map automatically.

Free, no adverts. Sign in with just your email address.
